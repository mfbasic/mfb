### 1. The "solid panel" example paints the foreground colour, not the background it sets
UNIT:      man-page:term/fillRect
CLAIM:     "Paint a solid panel, then a lighter band inside it:" followed by `term::setBackground(color::rgb(0, 0, 40))` and `term::fillRect(term::FillStyle.Filled, 1, 2, 12, 30)`

SUGGESTED: Change the example's set-up to `term::setForeground(color::rgb(0, 0, 40))` for the panel, and add one sentence to the description: "The glyph is drawn in the foreground colour; the background colour shows only through the gaps of the shade and checker patterns, so a `Filled` panel takes the colour set by `term::setForeground`, not `term::setBackground`."

### 2. "colours and attributes currently in effect" does not say which colour does what
UNIT:      man-page:term/fillRect
CLAIM:     "fills every cell of a rectangular region with a block or shade glyph chosen by the term::FillStyle enum, using the colours and attributes currently in effect."
VERDICT:   incomplete
EVIDENCE:  Probe at `/tmp/plan-125-scratch/D-phase6-current/man-page-term-fillRect/probe` with `setBold(TRUE)`, `setUnderline(TRUE)`, `setForeground(color::rgb(1,2,3))`: the stamped run is `ESC[1m ESC[4m ESC[38;2;1;2;3m ESC[48;2;0;0;0m ▞▞▞▞`, so bold, underline, foreground and background are all applied. True as far as it goes, but the page never says the glyph takes the foreground colour, which is the one fact a reader filling a "panel background" needs (see finding 1). The sibling sentence "The shade variants read as translucent overlays at a glance; the solid block is opaque" hints at it without stating which colour is on top.
SUGGESTED: "…using the colours and attributes currently in effect: the glyph is drawn in the foreground colour (with bold and underline if set) over the background colour, so `Filled` shows only the foreground and the shades and checkers mix the two."
